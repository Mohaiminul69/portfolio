Overview

Tuzy is the website of a tour management company that arranges tours and invites travellers from all over the world. It offers budget-friendly tour deals, visa assistance and hotel bookings.

There are also packages for adventure trips such as hiking and camping.

03

My bookings

See all the tours you’ve booked once you log in, and cancel any of them.

04

Booking management

Admins manage every booking, approving or cancelling them.

05

Admins can add brand-new tours to the site.
