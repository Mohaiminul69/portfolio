Overview

Frage is an online perfume shop. Its goal is simple: sell perfumes online. The store carries a wide range of perfumes, with separate collections for men and women at the premium end of the market.

What it does

01

Accounts

Log in to explore the full perfume collection.

02

Online orders

Order a perfume online. Your purchase details are saved to the database.

03

Order history

See all your previous orders once you log in, and cancel any of them.

04

Admin dashboard

A separate, password-protected dashboard where admins manage products, add new ones and handle orders. Customers can't see it.

05

Reviews

Customers can leave reviews on the site for everyone to read.
